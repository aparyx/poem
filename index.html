03 / A NOTE ON THE WORK

AD

A few
words.

I am just an ordinary man in this ordinary world.

I write when something stays with me long enough to become words. Sometimes it becomes a poem, sometimes a story, sometimes only a few lines. There is no schedule here; the archive grows when there is something to add.
